Using the mark
- Use the files as they are. Do not redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to the mark or the wordmark.
- Use the light files on light grounds and the dark files on dark grounds.
- Keep clear space round the mark and the lockup of at least half the ghost’s height.
- Show the mark at 16 px tall or more, and the lockup at 32 px tall or more.
- Write the name in lowercase as make.fun, with the dot, even at the start of a sentence.

The mascot as a token logo
- To put the mascot on a coin as its logo, use one of these 20 files as it is; all 20 are at https://make.fun/press/. The logo check accepts exactly these, the mascot alone:
  icon-1024.png, icon-512.png, icon-192.png, icon-96.png, avatar-500.png, apple-touch-icon.png,
  x/avatar-light-400.png, x/avatar-magenta-400.png, icon-maskable-192.png, icon-maskable-512.png,
  mark-1024.png, mark-on-dark-1024.png, favicon-32.png, discord/icon-1024.png,
  discord/icon-animated-512.gif, x/avatar-dark-400.png, x/avatar-face-400.png, icon-monochrome-512.png,
  mark.svg, mark-on-dark.svg
- Prefer icon-1024.png, 1024 x 1024 px: it is the largest square rendition, and the size to use when a platform asks for one logo image.
- The lockups, wordmarks, screenshots, banners and splash screens are not accepted as a token logo: they are compositions, not the mascot alone.

The referral end card
- Every make.fun social video post or ad ends with this card: 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, 16:9 or 9:16 for X. The field carries the poster's own make.fun referral code, typed in; never leave it empty and never use someone else's code.
- Your referral code is on the Referrals tab of your profile, https://make.fun/profile?tab=referrals; a referral link takes the form https://make.fun/r/<code>.
- Put the transparent card on the track above your clip, 2.5 seconds before it ends, or add the card on magenta after it.
- From that frame, blur the clip about 28 px and turn its saturation down, both easing in over about 0.8 seconds.
- Once the field comes to rest, type your own referral code where the caret blinks, in JetBrains Mono Bold or Inter Bold, in ink #191419.
- The 9:16 field rests 1.43 s into the transparent card. Its code starts at the caret, x 210 px, centred on y 1271 px of the 1080 x 1920 frame, at 62 px, with 510 px before the copy button.
- The 16:9 field rests 1.43 s into the transparent card. Its code starts at the caret, x 432 px, centred on y 733 px of the 1920 x 1080 frame, at 62 px, with 909 px before the copy button.
- The ProRes 4444 masters with alpha (.mov) are not in the kit: ask press@make.fun.
- For agents: https://make.fun/press/llms.txt says which file to use where and how to put the card on a video with ffmpeg; https://make.fun/press/manifest.json lists every file with its size and sha256.
